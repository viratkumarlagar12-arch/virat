## Formula to reuse
1. Hook = shock number + failure/twist in the first frame ("12 KM neeche aur phir drill haar gayi"), with a number graphic already on screen.
2. Name the thing only after the hook.
3. "Ab imagine karo" second-person descent; one visual change every ~6 s; numbers as yellow milestones.
4. One escalating variable (heat) with a HUD (ruler + thermometer).
5. Payoff line, then an open-ended mystery ending (drives comments and replays).
