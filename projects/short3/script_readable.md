# Leh mein dal kyun nahi galti? — final script (≈38 s voice + 2 s logo end card)

Hindi (for the voice) · Hinglish (for captions) · what we show on screen

**1.** लेह में दाल उबलती है...
   Leh mein dal ubalti hai...
   _Screen:_ 0-2 s hook: dark-space sky, snowy Leh peak; steel pot of dal boils hard on a flame, corner timer spins fast; big text 'LEH MEIN DAL...' slams in on the first word with an impact SFX + bubbling loop; no numbers yet.

**2.** फिर भी कच्ची क्यों?
   Phir bhi kachchi kyun?
   _Screen:_ Spoon lifts one still-hard lentil; 'KACHCHI KYUN?' completes the hook text with a red '?' on the lentil; small tick SFX; 'KACHCHI' is only a question hook here, nothing is claimed.

**3.** ए, ऑक्सीजन कम?
   A, oxygen kam?
   _Screen:_ Quiz card A 'OXYGEN KAM HONA' (O2 bubble) pops in on 'oxygen'; tick SFX; card stays in the safe zone; no numbers.

**4.** बी, ठंड?
   B, thand?
   _Screen:_ Quiz card B 'THAND' (snowflake) pops in next to A; tick SFX.

**5.** सी, प्रेशर कम?
   C, pressure kam?
   _Screen:_ Quiz card C 'PRESSURE KAM' (gauge) pops in; all three cards now sit in a row; tick SFX.

**6.** जवाब एंड में।
   Jawab end mein.
   _Screen:_ 'JAWAB END MEIN' stamp lands on the three cards, they dim and a lock icon snaps on; whoosh + stamp SFX; thin progress bar at the top starts filling until the answer.

**7.** लेह समुद्र से साढ़े तीन हज़ार मीटर ऊपर है।
   Leh samudra se saadhe teen hazaar meter upar hai.
   _Screen:_ Mountain with an altitude meter climbing from 0 to 'about 3,500 m' and a LEH pin (Leh town, not the airport) landing on 'upar hai'; rising-meter SFX; no pressure numbers yet.

**8.** वहाँ हवा का प्रेशर बस दो तिहाई।
   Wahan hawa ka pressure bas do tihaai.
   _Screen:_ Two bars: 'SEA LEVEL 100%' and 'LEH about 65% (~2/3)'; the Leh bar shrinks on 'do tihaai' with a punch-in; short thunk SFX.

**9.** पानी उबलता है, जब भाप हवा जितना ज़ोर लगाए।
   Paani ubalta hai, jab bhaap hawa jitna zor lagaye.
   _Screen:_ Pot cutaway: steam arrows push up, air arrows push down; the two sets grow until equal, then bubbles start; chip 'BHAAP KA ZOR = HAWA KA ZOR -> UBAAL'; soft bubble-pop SFX; no numbers.

**10.** वहाँ पानी सौ नहीं, लगभग अट्ठासी डिग्री पर उबलता है।
   Wahan paani sau nahi, lagbhag atthaasi degree par ubalta hai.
   _Screen:_ Big thermometer: left 'SEA LEVEL 100 C' (tiny 'normal pressure par'), right 'LEH about 88 C'; mercury rises and stops dead at 88, the 100 is slashed in red, gap tag '~12 C kam'; whole numbers only; punch-in + ding on 'atthaasi' (88 appears exactly then).

**11.** तेज़ आग से भाप बढ़ती है, तापमान नहीं।
   Tez aag se bhaap badhti hai, taapmaan nahi.
   _Screen:_ Flame under the pot grows and steam clouds swell, but the thermometer needle stays glued at about 88 C; chips 'KHULI DEG MEIN' and 'AAG TEZ = BHAAP ZYADA, TAPMAAN SAME'; flame whoosh + growing steam hiss.

**12.** इसीलिए दाल गलती है, पर बहुत देर में।
   Isiliye dal galti hai, par bahut der mein.
   _Screen:_ Two lentil bowls with running clocks, chip 'KHULI DEG MEIN' kept on: 'SEA LEVEL: jaldi' (lentils soften fast) vs 'LEH: DHEERE DHEERE GALTI HAI' (lentils go from hard to half-soft while the clock keeps running); no time multiple and no minutes on screen; slow tick SFX.

**13.** जवाब सी!
   Jawab C!
   _Screen:_ The quiz cards return, A and B grey out, C gets a green tick; win SFX + short flash (the one answer beat); punch-in on 'C'.

**14.** मुख्य वजह कम प्रेशर।
   Mukhya wajah kam pressure.
   _Screen:_ Label 'MUKHYA WAJAH: KAM PRESSURE' under the green C card; calm settle, no extra SFX.

**15.** ऑक्सीजन से उबाल तय नहीं होता, ठंड से भी नहीं।
   Oxygen se ubaal tay nahi hota, thand se bhi nahi.
   _Screen:_ Left: air pie chart labelled 'HAWA MEIN O2 KA HISSA about 21%' with the small note '(saans mein kam, par ubaal ka O2 se lena-dena nahi)', cross over A with the text 'UBAAL MEIN OXYGEN KA ROL NAHI'; right: two air thermometers (5 C and 30 C) while the pot water reads about 88 C in both, cross over B; chip 'UBAAL PRESSURE SE TAY HOTA HAI'; two soft thunk SFX; the pie appears on 'oxygen', the crosses land on 'nahi hota' and 'bhi nahi'.

**16.** इसका हल, प्रेशर कुकर।
   Iska hal, pressure cooker.
   _Screen:_ Pivot beat: a pressure cooker slides in with the tag 'ISKA HAL: PRESSURE COOKER'; the cards fade out; small whoosh.

**17.** लेह में कुकर के अंदर पानी लगभग एक सौ पंद्रह डिग्री तक पहुँचता है।
   Leh mein cooker ke andar paani lagbhag ek sau pandrah degree tak pahunchta hai.
   _Screen:_ Pressure cooker cutaway: lid sealed, steam trapped, gauge needle rises, inner thermometer climbs from 88 to 'about 115 C' on 'pandrah'; chips 'LEH: KHULI DEG about 88 C' vs 'LEH: COOKER about 115 C', a small 'sea level par about 120 C' chip and the line 'cooker ke hisaab se about 110-115 C'; never show 120 or 121 as the Leh number; whistle SFX.

**18.** समय कुछ बढ़ाओ, प्रेशर पूरा निकलने पर ही खोलो।
   Samay kuch badhao, pressure poora nikalne par hi kholo.
   _Screen:_ Calm beat: timer icon with '+ KUCH EXTRA TIME (cooker/dal ke hisaab se)' (no percentage); then the gauge needle falls to zero, a lock opens, green tick 'PRESSURE POORA NIKLE, TAB KHOLO'; music dips slightly; no hands on the valve.

**19.** ए, बी या सी?
   A, B ya C?
   _Screen:_ A / B / C buttons in a row pop in one by one on the three letters; comment bubble 'A, B ya C? Comment karo'.

**20.** कमेंट करो!
   Comment karo!
   _Screen:_ Comment bubble pulses; hold 0.5 s, dip to black, then the Science Unseen Hindi end card (~2 s: logo fade-in, slow push-in, fade-out); captions.until before the card, music fades out under it.
