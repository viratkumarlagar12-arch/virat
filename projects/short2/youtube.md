## Description (2259 chars, 2333 bytes; limit 5000 bytes)
What happens if you hold your breath? Ghutan oxygen ki kami se hoti hai ya CO2 se? A ya B - jawab video ke end mein.

Is Short mein tum seekhoge:
- Saans rokne par bechaini (urge to breathe) kyun shuru hoti hai
- CO2 paani se milkar blood ko thoda acidic kaise banata hai
- Brainstem (medulla) ke chemoreceptors is change ko kaise pakadte hain
- Breathing centre diaphragm ko "saans lo" ka signal kaise bhejta hai
- Ghutan ka main trigger kya hai - A ya B? (jawab end mein)

Science note: NCERT/textbooks ke hisaab se breath-hold mein bechaini ka sabse bada chemical signal badhta CO2 aur H+ hai; oxygen ki kami ka role chhota hota hai. Research (Parkes 2006) batati hai ki exact breakpoint kai signals se banta hai, jaise phephde/chest ka volume aur diaphragm ke sensors, aur iska ek simple jawab abhi nahi hai.

सांस रोकने पर घुटन क्यों होती है? CO2 बनाम ऑक्सीजन

Class 11 connection: yeh topic NCERT Class 11 Biology ke chapter "Breathing and Exchange of Gases" se juda hai. Yeh sirf ek chhota intro hai, poora chapter nahi. Exam ke liye apni NCERT book hi follow karo. Yeh channel NCERT se affiliated nahi hai.

Safety note: Yeh video sirf padhai aur jaankari ke liye hai, medical advice nahi. 10 second ka try karna ho to baithkar, halke se karo; gaadi chalate ya paani ke paas bilkul nahi, aur chakkar aaye to turant saans lo. Tez saansein (hyperventilation) lekar ya paani mein saans rokne ki practice kabhi mat karo - isse ghutan der se lagti hai aur behoshi bina warning aa sakti hai (shallow-water blackout). Lambe breath-hold ka challenge mat lo. Health ki koi problem ho to doctor se baat karo.
Safety source: StatPearls, "Shallow Water Blackout" (Bart RM, Murray BP, Lau H.), NCBI Bookshelf: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK554620/
